# PMCR-O Convergence

Convergence is a governed stopping condition, not simply exhaustion of cycles.

## A Seed Intent is converged when

- the intended outcome is sufficiently explicit for execution or closure,
- material constraints and dependencies are understood,
- relevant changes have been checked,
- no known unresolved contradiction materially affects the objective,
- and the next cycle would not be expected to materially improve the operational interpretation.

## Non-converged outcomes

`refine` — additional PMCR-O work should produce a better Seed Intent.

`blocked` — progress requires an external dependency, missing capability, or human decision.

`complete` — the Goal has been achieved and no next Seed Intent is required.

`superseded` — the current Seed Intent is no longer the correct operational path, and the Reflector has established a replacement.

## Important distinction

Convergence is not proof of metaphysical or psychological “true intent.” It means the system has reached a sufficiently resolved operational intent for the Goal under the available evidence and governance constraints.
