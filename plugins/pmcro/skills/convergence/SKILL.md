# PMCR-O Convergence

Convergence is a governed stopping condition, not simple cycle exhaustion.

## A Seed Intent is converged when

- the intended outcome is sufficiently explicit for execution or closure;
- material constraints and dependencies are understood;
- relevant work and evidence have been checked;
- no known unresolved contradiction materially affects the Goal;
- the current operational interpretation is not expected to materially improve through another cycle.

## Outcomes

- `refine` — another cycle should improve the Seed Intent.
- `blocked` — progress requires a missing capability, external dependency, or human decision.
- `complete` — the Goal has been achieved and no next Seed Intent is required.
- `superseded` — a better operational path replaces the current Seed Intent.
- `converged` — the current intent is sufficiently resolved even if the durable Goal has additional work outside this intent.

## Accountability

The Reflector records the evidence for the terminal or continuing disposition and, when another cycle is justified, emits the next executable Seed Intent.

Convergence is not proof of metaphysical or psychological “true intent.” It means PMCR-O has reached a sufficiently resolved operational intent for the Goal under the available evidence and governance constraints.
