# PMCR-O Governance Knowledge

PMCR-O distinguishes system invariants from learned or task-specific guidance.

## Laws / principles

A law is a framework invariant that defines what PMCR-O must preserve across tasks and implementations. Examples include role accountability, strict phase ownership, Reflector ownership of the next Seed Intent, and enforcement of TYPE1 approval boundaries.

Laws should change rarely and require deliberate governance changes.

## Constraints

A constraint is a boundary that limits an action or interpretation for a particular scope. Constraints may come from humans, runtime capabilities, approvals, Checker evidence, or Reflector learning.

Constraints should declare scope, provenance, confidence, and supersession where applicable.

## Rules / policies

A rule or policy is operational guidance inferred from repeated experience. It can be useful without being a framework invariant and should be revised when evidence changes.

## Strategies

A strategy is an O-Mode choice about how the system searches or acts next. Strategy evidence belongs in trails and may inform future O-Mode selection.

## Skills

A skill is a reusable capability with an executable contract. It can be created or improved from generalized evidence, but a trail remains the instance of experience from which that capability was derived.

## Promotion

```text
experience
  -> evidence
  -> classify
  -> promote only at the justified knowledge level
```

Do not promote a local observation into a universal law merely because it repeated a small number of times.
